## Key Features

- Planning shows virtually in offline mode
- Frequency coordination and assignment
- Spectrum analyzer functionality
- Multi-channel system control
- Firmware updates for transmitters and receivers
- Compatible with Digital 6000, Digital 9000, EW-DX, EW-D, and legacy systems
